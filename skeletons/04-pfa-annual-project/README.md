# 04 — PFA (Projet de Fin d'Année)

**Use this when:** you are in 3rd or 4th year and submitting an end-of-year
project, whether done in a company or at the school.

## How a PFA differs from a PFE

Structurally they are close. The differences are of degree, and they are the
differences a jury is actually grading against:

| | PFA | PFE |
|---|---|---|
| Year | 3rd or 4th | Final year |
| Autonomy expected | Guided; supervisor sets direction | Near-autonomous; you set direction |
| État de l'art | Brief — situate the problem | Substantial — position the contribution |
| Scope | One well-defined component or feature set | A complete project |
| Length | 25–40 pages | 50–80 pages |
| Weight on the diploma | A module grade | Defended before a jury; decisive |

The single most common PFA mistake is imitating a PFE — a forty-page état de
l'art on a project that took eight weeks. The examiner reads that as padding.

**Write less état de l'art and more about what you actually built and learned.**
A PFA that clearly explains one component well beats a PFA that gestures at a
whole system.

## Shape

Three or four chapters, 25–40 pages.

| Chapter | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1. Contexte et cadrage | What problem, and what was asked of you? |
| 2. Étude et conception | What did you consider, and what did you choose? |
| 3. Réalisation | What did you build, and does it work? |
| 4. Bilan *(optional, can be folded into the conclusion)* | What did you learn? |

If the PFA is research-flavoured (a model, an experiment), take chapters 2 to 4
of `02-pfe-research-ml` and compress them; keep the reproducibility content, drop
the ablations.
