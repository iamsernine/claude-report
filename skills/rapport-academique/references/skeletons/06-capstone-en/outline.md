# Outline — IT capstone report (English)

---

## Front matter

- Cover page
- Title page
- Approval sheet *(if your programme requires one)*
- Acknowledgements
- Dedication *(optional)*
- **Abstract** — 150–250 words: objective, methodology, results, main conclusions
- Keywords — 5 to 7
- Table of contents
- List of tables
- List of figures
- List of abbreviations
