# 00 — Stage d'initiation / stage ouvrier

**Use this when:** first-year or second-year observation internship, typically
2 to 6 weeks. The student observes; they are not expected to deliver anything.

**Length:** 10–20 pages.

## What makes this different from every other skeleton

There is no problématique and there is no contribution. Trying to manufacture
either is the defining mistake of these reports — a student who spent three weeks
shadowing a network team writes "notre solution permet d'optimiser…" and the
instructor sees straight through it.

What is actually being graded:

- Did you understand what the organisation does and how it is structured?
- Can you describe a technical environment accurately?
- Did you observe carefully and report honestly?
- Can you write a clean, well-formed document?

That last one matters more than people expect. This is often the first formal
document a student produces, and the grade is substantially a writing grade.

## Shape

| Section | Answers |
|---|---|
| 1. Présentation de l'organisme | Where were you, and what do they do? |
| 2. Environnement technique | What did they work with? |
| 3. Déroulement du stage | What did you observe and do, week by week? |
| 4. Apports du stage | What did you learn? |

## Honest framing beats invented achievement

"J'ai assisté aux interventions de maintenance sans y participer directement, ce
qui m'a permis d'observer…" reads as maturity. "J'ai mis en place une solution
de supervision" for a three-week observation internship reads as fiction, and the
instructor will ask a question you cannot answer.
