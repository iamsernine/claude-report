# Sources

The conventions in this repository were assembled from published guides and from
a sample of real IT project reports from Moroccan and Tunisian institutions.

## Institutional writing guides

- Université Cadi Ayyad, Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Safi — *Guide de rédaction
  et de présentation orale d'un projet de fin d'études* (H. Ait Malek, 2018).
  Source of the mandatory/optional front-matter table, the 1/5–3/5–1/5 proportion
  rule, the 30-page ceiling, typography, pagination, and the defense grading
  formula.
- EPI / PEV Group — *Guide de préparation du rapport de PFE*, aligned with ASIIN
  accreditation requirements. Source of the four-chapter market-study variant,
  the 150–250 word abstract with 5–7 keywords, 2.5 cm margins, chapter separator
  pages, and formula numbering.
- ISET Jendouba and ISET *Guide d'élaboration d'un rapport de PFE* (T. Naili) —
  framing of the PFE as a technical, decision-oriented document.

## Report corpora consulted

- FST Fès (memoirepfe.fst-usmba.ac.ma) — génie informatique and data science PFEs.
- ENSA Oujda, ENSET Mohammedia, ENSIAS — chapter-plan conventions.
- Tunisian engineering reports using the 2TUP process (functional / technical /
  design-and-realisation branches).
- Polytech Lille P44 IronCar report — structure of an embedded deep-learning
  project with an explicit "état de l'art et positionnement par rapport à
  l'existant" section.

## Anglophone capstone conventions

- RIT Software Engineering — capstone vs. thesis distinction (depth of background
  research and justification).
- Standard IT capstone outline: introduction, existing systems, problem analysis,
  software requirements, design, testing, implementation, project legacy, user
  manual, source code, bibliography.

## A note on precedence

Where this repository and your department's template disagree, the department
wins. These skeletons are a starting structure and a checklist, not a regulation.
