# Outline — PFA

Aim for 25–40 pages excluding annexes. Resist expanding.

---

## Front matter

- Page de garde
- Remerciements
- Résumé + mots-clés (an English abstract is a nice extra, not expected)
- Sommaire
- Liste des figures / tableaux — only if there are more than a handful
- Liste des acronymes — only if needed

## Introduction générale — 1 page

Context, problem, objectives, plan. One page. Not two.

---

## Chapitre 1 — Contexte et cadrage du projet

### 1.1 Cadre du projet
- Host organisation *or* academic framing. If academic, this is two paragraphs,
  not a section.

### 1.2 Étude de l'existant
- What exists today and what is wrong with it. Keep it concrete.

### 1.3 Problématique et objectifs
- Scope, and what is explicitly out of scope. Being clear about the boundary is
  what makes a small project look deliberate rather than incomplete.

### 1.4 Organisation du travail
- Method, planning, tools. A short Gantt is enough.

### Conclusion du chapitre

---

## Chapitre 2 — Étude et conception

### 2.1 Étude des solutions envisageables
- Two or three options, compared on criteria that matter here
- The choice and its justification

### 2.2 Spécification des besoins
- Functional requirements, numbered
- Non-functional requirements — a short list, but with real numbers

### 2.3 Conception
- Architecture, in one diagram
- The one or two models that matter (class diagram, data schema, pipeline
  diagram — whichever fits the project)

### Conclusion du chapitre

---

## Chapitre 3 — Réalisation et validation

### 3.1 Environnement et outils
### 3.2 Technologies utilisées et justification
### 3.3 Mise en œuvre
- The parts that were actually hard. Skip the boilerplate.
### 3.4 Résultats
- Screenshots, measurements, or experimental results, depending on the project
### 3.5 Tests et validation
- A test table is enough at this level
### Conclusion du chapitre

---

## Conclusion générale et perspectives — 1 to 2 pages

- What was achieved against the objectives
- Difficulties and how they were handled
- Limitations
- Perspectives
- **Bilan personnel** — what you learned, technically and otherwise. This carries
  real weight in a PFA in a way it does not in a PFE.

---

## Back matter

- Bibliographie / Webographie
- Annexes — keep them short
