# Formatting standards

Two conventions dominate Moroccan and Tunisian engineering schools. Both are
defensible. **Check your department's own template first** — it always wins over
anything in this repo.

| | UCA-style (common in Moroccan universities) | ASIIN-aligned (accredited engineering programmes) |
|---|---|---|
| Body font | Times New Roman 12 pt | Times New Roman 12 pt |
| Line spacing | 1.5 body; single for TOC, lists, abstract, notes, bibliography | 1.5 |
| Margins | 2 cm all sides + 0.5 cm binding on the left | 2.5 cm all sides; header 1.0 cm, footer 1.0 cm |
| Footnotes | 10 pt when body is 12 pt | 10 pt, 11 pt spacing, justified, numbered 1..n |
| Header | Section title | Chapter title, max ~40 characters |
| Footer | Author, year, degree, institution | Page number |

## Heading hierarchy

| Level | Size | Style | Numbering |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chapter | 16 pt | bold | `1.` |
| Section | 14 pt | bold | `1.1`, `1.2` |
| Sub-section | 12 pt | bold | `1.1.1` |
| Deeper | 12 pt | italic | `a)` or `1-` |

Headings are left-aligned and decimally numbered. Never underline a heading.
Never leave a heading stranded at the bottom of a page. To emphasise a term in
running text use italic or bold — never underline.

## Pagination

- The title page carries no number.
- Preliminary pages may use lowercase roman numerals (i, ii, iii).
- Arabic numerals start at the **general introduction** and run continuously
  through the bibliography.
- Page number sits at the bottom right.
- Insert a separator page before each chapter.

## Figures and tables

- Number consecutively in arabic numerals, in order of first mention
  (Figure 1, Figure 2 / Tableau 1, Tableau 2).
- **Figure captions go below** the figure. Table captions: the UCA guide places
  them below, most IT departments and the ASIIN-aligned guides place them
  **above**. Pick one, apply it everywhere.
- Caption in Times New Roman 12 pt, centred. Leave one blank line above and two
  below each caption.
- Place the figure or table immediately after the paragraph that first mentions
  it; if there is no room, put it at the top of the following page.
- Every illustration must be cited and justified in the running text.
- Reference by number — "the values in Table 1.3" — never "the table below" or
  "on page 10".
- Never present the same data as both a table and a figure.
- Any illustration taken from a source must carry that source in its caption.

## Formulas

Numbered in parentheses from (1) to (n), right-aligned on the first or last line.

## Abstract and keywords

Two conventions:

- **UCA**: ~150 words, maximum 5 keywords.
- **ASIIN**: 150–250 words covering objective, methodology, results and main
  conclusions; 5–7 keywords chosen so the topic is discoverable in academic
  databases.

Always include an English title, abstract and keywords alongside the French
ones — it materially increases the report's visibility.

## Citations

Either works, as long as it is applied consistently:

- **Numbered** — `[1]`, `[2]` in order of appearance. This is the natural choice
  for IT and AI work; IEEE style is the usual concrete form.
- **Author–date (APA)** — `(Trottier, Laforce & Cloutier, 1998)`.

Short quotations (up to three lines) sit inline in guillemets. Longer ones are
detached, single-spaced, indented 1 cm, without guillemets.

Use a reference manager (Zotero, JabRef, BibTeX) from day one. Reformatting 60
references by hand the night before submission is a known way to lose marks.

## Annexes

Identified by capital letters (Annexe A, Annexe B), each with a title, each
announced in the body text ("voir Annexe A"). Give them local pagination.
